02 / CASE FILES

Three risk management cases

The US and UK cases have developments this week. The Danish case adds published quantified results.

01 / United States47chemicals over the reporting threshold
Settlement announced 2026.11.04

Colgate-Palmolive files missing import chemical data

In March 2023 the EPA found unreported 2016–2019 import data; with EPA help the company filed the reports that July. This week the EPA announced a settlement, and the company has paid a US$930,000 penalty. Completing the data is the confirmed result; the public material gives no change in emissions or exposure.

Practices to take back to site Author's analysis

The notice does not explain the company's internal root cause. Start with one high-volume material and have HSE, procurement and customs teams check substance identity, import volumes, thresholds and deadlines, making sure every filing traces back to original records.

02 / United Kingdom75,000reef cubes deployed (approx.)
Ecological monitoring update 2026.11.02

Rampion verifies scour protection and ecological benefit separately

There had been a scour pit about 2–3 m deep near the J04 turbine foundation. The team first ran simulated storm and quayside logistics tests, then deployed the cubes from a fall-pipe vessel in October 2025. An industry report on 16 Oct 2026 said the deployment stayed stable through its first winter. The ecological survey covering 7 turbine positions was delayed by sea conditions, its window extended to 1 Dec; biodiversity benefits are still being monitored.

Practices to take back to site Author's analysis

In management of change, set separate verification indicators for engineering stability and environmental effect, compare with conventional rock placement and unprotected positions, and build weather delays and re-mobilisation conditions into the work plan.

03 / Denmark85%+accident reduction recorded in the case
Good Practice Awards 2023–2025 · Existing case

GSV ties incident reports to response deadlines

After acquiring four companies, GSV faced the challenge of integrating safety management in 2019. It set up an OSH team, introduced mandatory annual training and mobile hazard reporting with photos; incident reports automatically notify the safety department and must be handled within 36 hours. EU-OSHA records that compared with 2019, accidents fell by more than 85% and working time lost to accidents fell by 86%. The contribution of each measure is not broken out, and no new results were found this week.

Practices to take back to site Author's analysis

Trial it with your existing forms on one recurring type of problem: assign a handler to every report and record the first response and the on-site verification separately. This existing case is a useful reference for traditional equipment service firms integrating their reporting process.

03 / FIELD APPLICATION

Checks from this issue to take back to site

Author's analysis

Emergency power failure scenarios
Using the Visund investigation as a follow-up item, list the functions that must stay available if emergency switchboards fail, and the drill evidence for them. The cause of the incident is still under investigation.
Accepting improvement measures
Before approving an improvement, record the verification indicators, baseline, re-test date and owner.
