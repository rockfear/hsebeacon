02 / Major accident tracker

Aliağa steel plant, Turkey: overhead crane falls and explosion during a maintenance lift

Under investigation

OSHA Taiwan published an accident summary on 13 Nov. The accident happened on 9 Nov. According to initial statements by local officials, while the electric arc furnace shell was being lifted, the crane runway beam came off its support. Falling objects may have ruptured natural gas and oxygen lines, followed by an explosion. Why the runway beam came loose, the full accident mechanism and responsibility await a formal investigation.

On casualties, trade media reported on 10 Nov that 17 workers died and 12 were injured; another source records 18 deaths. Final figures are those officially released by Turkish authorities.

Author's analysis When reviewing a lifting plan, check which energised equipment or pipelines a falling load would hit if the load or its support failed.

03 / CASE FILES

Three improvement cases from different regions

The US and English cases use results published this week. The Taiwan case was published on 3 Nov and was chosen because it gives before-and-after concentrations for the same task. None of the three was covered in depth in the previous two issues.

01 / Taiwan<0.888ppm methanol after redesign (was 15.95 ppm)
Published 2026.11.03 · Engineering fix

Methanol dip coating: moving exhaust capture next to the emission source

Background and challenge.Methanol evaporates during dip coating. The design had to balance working space with vapour capture. The team used a rear slot hood with local enclosure to capture contaminants near the source. OSHA Taiwan reported on 3 Nov 2026 that methanol concentration fell from 15.95 ppm to below 0.888 ppm.

How to read it.The summary does not describe the full sampling conditions, so these figures say nothing about every shift, work position or long-term personal exposure. OSHA Taiwan's industrial ventilation section separately covers equipment design, performance checks and maintenance.

A first step to borrow Author's analysis

Before the redesign, record the operations most prone to release, where operators stand and the workload. After it, re-measure under comparable conditions and keep a baseline for future re-tests.

02 / United States~2 yearsEPA estimate to reach source area goals (original remedy: 15+ years)
Announced 2026.11.12 · Cleanup goals met

York former dry-cleaning site: sampling confirms cleanup goals are met

Background and challenge.Old dry-cleaning operations left tetrachloroethylene (PCE) contamination in soil source areas and a groundwater plume. The EPA used in-situ thermal remediation, heating the soil to vaporise contaminants and then treating the vapour, to limit the disruption that large-scale excavation would cause to the neighbourhood and local businesses.

Results announced on 12 Nov.At the Seventh Street source area (OU 01), every sampling location and depth met the PCE soil goals set in the Record of Decision. The Fifth Street source area (OU 02) met its goals in 2025, and work now turns to the groundwater plume (OU 03). The EPA estimates thermal remediation took about two years to reach source area goals, against more than 15 years estimated for the original remedy. That time difference compares estimates for two methods; the confirmed result is that soil samples met the goals.

A first step to borrow Author's analysis

Set closure conditions by area and by medium. For each stage, write down what to measure, where, and what standard counts as done.

03 / England−30%PFOS in rivers, 2016–2025
Published 2026.11.12 · Long-term monitoring

PFAS management: verifying environmental change with years of monitoring

Background and challenge.PFAS cover many substances, and both past use and ongoing emissions can affect concentrations. Beyond existing controls, the Environment Agency widened the range of water bodies and substances it monitors and improved its analytical methods. The formal assessment published on 12 Nov 2026 updates its 2021 review.

Long-term results observed.From 2016 to 2025, PFOS in rivers fell by about 30% and PFOA by about 29%. From 2016 to 2024, PFOS in freshwater fish fell by about 49%. The official assessment considers existing restrictions and government action to have helped. How much each measure contributed cannot be read from the trend figures, and the report notes PFAS remain widespread.

A first step to borrow Author's analysis

In your tracking sheet, record the substance, sample type and observation period. If sampling locations or methods change, record the date of the change and the existing baseline.
