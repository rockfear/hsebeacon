02 / CASE FILES

Three risk management cases

01 / Taiwan305workers given special health examinations
Published 2026.10.27 · Survey results

Engineered stone processing dust

A cross-agency survey visited 60 workplaces and helped 305 workers get health checks, finding 4 with suspected work-related lung abnormalities. The survey gives early identification; actual exposure improvement still needs follow-up.

Management approach and field practice Author's analysis

Link site visits with health surveillance. First check whether wet processing and local exhaust ventilation are actually used, then compare exposure monitoring before and after improvements, and follow up on referrals for abnormal results.

OSHA Taiwan survey summary ↗
02 / United States2025SHARP recognition
Existing improvement case · Equipment decommissioning

A-Line TDS's daily safety meetings

Since 2022 this transformer dismantler has used on-site consultation and lets employees raise concerns at daily safety meetings. OSHA records no recordable incidents in high-risk work such as dismantling; the public material gives no reporting period or total hours worked.

Management approach and field practice Author's analysis

Use open discussion to surface job problems, then give each important item an owner, a deadline and a way to verify it on site. When reading near-miss counts, look at willingness to report and exposure as well.

03 / Ireland60 kgbarrel weight that used to be unloaded by hand
Published 2025.05.14 · Engineering fix

Midleton Distillery switches to robotic barrel unloading

Accidents continued after job rotation, lower stacking and training. HSE, engineering, operations, operators and the supplier jointly designed an AI vision-guided robot, fully running from summer 2024.

Management approach and field practice Author's analysis

Light curtains, scanners and interlocks manage human-robot interaction. The EU-OSHA case records no reported barrel handling accidents after introduction, a result limited to that task and observation period. The annual saving of about €1.38 million is an estimate.

03 / FIELD APPLICATION

Four checks to take back to site

Author's analysis

Work with multiple contractors
In RAMS / PTW review, check the timing, scope, isolation conditions and owner of simultaneous operations.
Equipment decommissioning
Confirm residual energy, materials and waste routes before dismantling, and keep control records.
Chronic exposure
Put exposure monitoring results, exceedance fixes and health abnormality follow-up side by side.
Intervening in automated equipment
Verify safe conditions for clearing jams, cleaning, maintenance and restarting.
